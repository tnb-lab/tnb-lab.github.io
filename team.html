Alberto Granzotto

Principal Investigator

Associate Professor in molecular neuroscience at CAST and DNISC, Università “G. d’Annunzio” Chieti–Pescara. His work covers zinc signaling, neuroinflammation, iPSC-derived microglia and neurodegeneration, from human cells to in vivo chimeric xenotransplantation models. He holds an MSCA Global Fellowship.
