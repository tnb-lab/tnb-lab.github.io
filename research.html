Research

01 iPSC-derived models of neurodegenerative disease

We reprogram cells into induced pluripotent stem cells (iPSCs) and differentiate them into human neurons and microglia, the immune cells of the brain. These models let us study Alzheimer’s disease and related disorders in human cells, and we test them in vivo by transplanting human microglia into the mouse brain (xenotransplantation).

02 Zinc signaling

Zinc is an essential trace metal and also a signaling ion in the brain. We investigate how zinc crosses cell membranes through dedicated transporters, and how these fluxes shape the inflammatory response of microglia.

03 Neuronal resilience

In many neurodegenerative diseases some neuron populations die while others survive. A classic example is the group of neurons marked by NADPH-diaphorase activity, which are spared in several conditions. We ask what makes them resistant, aiming to find protective mechanisms that could be harnessed therapeutically.
