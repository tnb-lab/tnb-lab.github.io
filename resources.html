  • Protocols folder (members) Step-by-step lab protocols.
  • Reagent inventory (members) Current stock, lot numbers and storage locations.
  • Antibody inventory (members) Current stock, lot numbers and storage locations.
  • Cell line inventory (members) iPSC lines, differentiation batches and freezer map.
  • Equipment booking and SOPs (members) Instrument schedules and standard operating procedures.
  • Templates and forms (members; in preparation) Lab notebook templates, ordering forms, onboarding checklist.
  • The mouse brain in stereotaxic coordinates Interactive rat and mouse brain atlases with stereotaxic coordinates. An essential reference for the mouse brain or related species.
  • Allen Brain Map Human and mouse brain atlases, including single-cell and spatial transcriptomics.
  • Human Protein Atlas Protein and RNA expression across human tissues, cells and brain regions.
  • GTEx Portal Gene expression and regulation across human tissues.
  • Brain RNA-Seq Cell-type-specific gene expression in mouse and human brain.
  • Alzforum Mutations Database Curated variants linked to Alzheimer's disease and other neurodegenerative disorders.
  • Fiji (ImageJ) Open-source platform for microscopy image analysis.
  • MATLAB & Simulink MATLAB is a computing platform for scientific applications like data analysis, signal and image processing. The lab has access to the institutional Total Academic Headcount (TAH) license. Anyone with a (at)unich.it account can download and install the software. MATLAB is already installed on the lab Windows workstations and can be accessed with your institutional credentials.
  • OriginPro Proprietary software for graphing and data analysis. The lab has a OriginPro 2026b license. The software is available on a Windows workstation (ask Alberto for access).
  • YASARA YASARA is a molecular-graphics, modeling and simulation program. The lab has a group leader multiple license for YASARA Structure. The software is available on 3 high-end workstations on both Windows and Linux environments.
  • Zotero Free reference manager.
  • Open Science Framework (OSF) Project management, preregistration and data sharing.
  • Zenodo Repository for datasets, code and supplementary material, with DOIs.
  • bioRxiv Preprint server for biology.
  • protocols.io Open, citable and versioned protocols.
  • FAIR principles Making data findable, accessible, interoperable and reusable.
  • ARRIVE guidelines Reporting standards for animal research.
  • NC3Rs Replacement, reduction and refinement in animal research.
  • DORA Declaration on Research Assessment: evaluating research on its merits.
  • Sherpa Romeo Journal open-access and self-archiving policies.
